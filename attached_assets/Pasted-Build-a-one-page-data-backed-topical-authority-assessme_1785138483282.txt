Build a one-page, data-backed topical authority assessment for

FRAMEWORK — operationalize this, don't just cite it
Topical Authority = Topical Coverage x Historical Data.
Coverage = complete, structured, CONNECTED information. Page count is
not coverage. Historical Data = quality of engagement over time, and it
lags roughly 6 months — so today's position reflects work from at least
two quarters ago. Every metric below maps to one side of that equation.

Assign each GSC/Bing query to Entity A, Entity B, both, or neither.

  ai brand monitoring
- Entity B seeds: generative engine optimization, geo, aeo,
  answer engine optimization, llm seo, ai seo, optimize for chatgpt /
  perplexity / ai overviews

STEP 2 — METRICS, per entity, per engine (Google vs Bing side by side,
never summed, never averaged)

Historical Data signals:
- Impressions, clicks, CTR, avg position (weighted by impressions, not
  a mean of means)
- 6-month trend on each, since authority lags roughly two quarters
- GA4 for the matched landing pages: organic sessions, engagement rate,
  avg engagement time, conversions

Coverage signals (this is what "depth" means here — override if you
meant crawl depth):
- Query depth: count of DISTINCT queries with >=1 impression in the
  cluster. This is the primary coverage proxy.
- Long-tail share: % of cluster impressions from queries of 4+ words
- Page spread: count of distinct URLs earning impressions in the
  cluster, and the Gini/concentration of impressions across them
- Intent spread: bucket cluster queries by predicate (know / compare /
  buy / do) and show the distribution
- URL depth: directory level of each ranking URL from root

- Core vs outer: which URLs carry commercial intent queries (core) vs
