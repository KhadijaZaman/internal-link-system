**How to apply:** Use performance evidence to rank pages within the core, but treat core role as an eligibility gate. Evidence-backed central pages stay Tier 1; adjacent pages cap at Tier 3 and outside-core pages at Tier 4. Neither can be low-hanging AI Visibility wins. Preserve one primary cluster per page and retain off-core metrics for context.
